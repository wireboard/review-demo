About this site

Juniper Tea is not a real shop. This site exists so that WireBoard and the people reviewing its integrations can see real visits arrive: every page you open here shows up in the WireBoard dashboard of the demo account, live.

The buttons send custom events (category shop or engagement), so the event reports have something to show too.

Want this on your own site? Try WireBoard.

Analytics by WireBoard in cookieless mode: no cookies and no local storage, only anonymous visit statistics.
